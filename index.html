About Guidonica

High-performance, client-only web tool for sight-reading and solfège practice. Inspired by Guido d'Arezzo's manus guidonica and historic musical pedagogy, Guidonica continuously streams procedurally generated music notation across a fixed playhead in synchronization with a Web Audio synthesized metronome.
