## Relation to the AGPL

The AGPL is a copyright license. It covers the code and the logo files as works of authorship, but it grants **no rights to use the trademarks**. Section 7(e) of the AGPL lets a licensor add a term "declining to grant rights under trademark law for use of some trade names, trademarks, or service marks". This file is the notice of that additional term for Guidonica.

In practice: you can fork and change Guidonica freely under the AGPL. If you share your changed version, give it your own name and logo.

## Allowed without asking

- **Unmodified copies.** Redistributing, mirroring or self-hosting the official, unmodified source or builds under the Guidonica name, for personal, classroom or institutional use.
- **Factual references.** Saying that your project is "based on Guidonica" or "a fork of Guidonica", or that it is compatible with it, as long as your own name and logo come first and are clearly different.
- **Talking about Guidonica.** Articles, reviews, videos, teaching material, slides and links that name or show Guidonica.

## Rebranding a fork

If you publish a modified version, replace the marks before you share it:

- `index.html`: the `<title>`, the header `<h1>`, the meta description, the social-preview tags, the social profile links and their `rel="me"` verification links.
- `src/i18n/landing.ts`: the titles and descriptions of the `/it/`, `/fr/`, `/de/` and `/es/` landing pages.
- `public/manifest.webmanifest`: `name` and `short_name`.
- The icons: `public/favicon.*`, `public/icon-*.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `docs/brand/` and the inline header glyph. They are generated by `scripts/build-icons.mjs`, so change the script or replace its output.
- The social-preview card `public/og-image.png`, and the banners and trailer still in `docs/brand/`.
- The "Guidonica" strings in `src/i18n/locales/*.ts` (title, about dialog, intro, tips) and in the release notes `CHANGELOG.md` and `src/i18n/changelog/*.md`.
- `public/CNAME`, `public/robots.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml` and `public/.well-known/security.txt`: point them to your own domain and contact, or remove them.
- `.github/FUNDING.yml`: your own sponsorship links, or remove it.

Keep the AGPL notices and the copyright lines, and include an attribution such as **"based on Guidonica by A. C. Lo Cascio"**. The music font subset may keep its internal name "Guidonica Notation": it is a technical font name, not a use of the mark as the name of your product.
